title: Surgeries, Seiberg-Witten and smooth structures

location: HFG 707
speaker: Bas Wensink
abstract: >
  In this talk, we will see how Seiberg-Witten invariants
  change under certain four-manifold surgeries: blow-ups and
  logarithmic transformations. Logarithmic transformations can be used
  to obtain a family of smooth four-manifolds called "Dolgachev
  surfaces", which are homeomorphic, but have different Seiberg-Witten
  invariants, so they are not diffeomorphic.
