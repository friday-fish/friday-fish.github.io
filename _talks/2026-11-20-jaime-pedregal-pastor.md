title: "Higher holonomy for higher Lie algebroids on graded vector bundles: Part 2"
abstract: "I will build on Part 1 of this series to explain how to construct higher parallel transport functors. If classical parallel transport is the “integration” of the connection along paths, higher parallel transport will be the “integration” of the higher connection along higher simplices. We will use these functors to define the higher holonomy groups of a higher connection. The definition of these groups is a bit subtle, since the obvious thing to try does not give a higher group, because this is too weak a notion. Instead, we change the focus from higher groups to simplicial groups, which carry more structure and are better suited to our situation."
