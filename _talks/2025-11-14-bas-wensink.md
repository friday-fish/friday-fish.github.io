abstract: >
  In this talk, we will introduce differential operators on manifolds and give some basic
  results about elliptic operators (and elliptic complexes). The goal is to prove that every $L^{2,2}$-solution of the Seiberg-Witten equations is gauge-equivalent to a smooth one and to compute the expected dimension of the Seiberg-Witten moduli space.
