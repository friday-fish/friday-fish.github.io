title: The Seiberg-Witten equations on cylinders
start_time: '12:30'
end_time: '14:30'
location: HFG 707
speaker: Bas Wensink
abstract: >
  In this talk, we will see the Seiberg-Witten equations on cylinders and applications to the
  topology of four-manifolds. One application is the result that connected sums of four-manifolds
  with $b^+>0$ have vanishing Seiberg-Witten invariants, implying that symplectic four-manifolds are
  irreducible. Another application will be the minimal genus problem in four-dimensions:
  holomorphic curves in Kähler surfaces are genus minimizing in their homology class, and more
  generally, symplectic surfaces in symplectic four-manifolds are genus minimizing.
