title: Boundary, spectral asymmetry, and the Atiyah-Patodi-Singer index theorem
start_time: '11:00'

speaker: Ashkan Sadat Kyaee
abstract: >
  In this talk we will dive into the journey of Atiyah, Patodi and
  Singer as they try to tackle the problem of extending the index formula to the
  case of manifolds with boundary. In the closed case, the Atiyah–Singer index
  theorem expresses the index of an elliptic operator in terms of local
  curvature invariants. But in the presence of a boundary, this locality breaks
  down and they make a surprising discovery: the missing global boundary
  contribution is spectral in nature.

  We will begin by illustrating the problem through the example of the
  Hirzebruch signature theorem and by introducing the eta function. We then
  define the spectral boundary conditions and study the cylindrical model near
  the boundary for first-order elliptic differential operators. Above all, we
  will understand how the eta invariant emerges from the heat kernel asymptotics
  and appears as the boundary correction term in the APS index formula. Finally,
  we will briefly discuss some further results concerning the eta function and
  its regularity in more general settings.
