abstract: >
  Imagine that you have a nested manifold: a small manifold
  inside a big manifold. You know that the big manifold is a boundary
  and that the small manifold is a boundary. With this information,
  when can you assert that your nested manifold is a nested boundary?
  We delve in this problem via a nested Pontryagin-Thom construction:
  the study of nested manifolds up to cobordism amounts to the study
  of homotopy groups of... bananas! I will discuss this result and
  some of its surprising consequences.
