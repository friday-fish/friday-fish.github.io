title: "Higher holonomy for higher Lie algebroids on graded vector bundles: Part 1"
abstract: "Classical holonomy on vector bundles concerns itself with the parallel transport of connection on vector bundles along paths. In this talk, I will explain ingredients necessary to generalize parallel transport to connections of (higher) Lie algebroids. This generalized notion of parallel transport does not only act through paths but also through higher dimensional simplices. Furthermore, this parallel transport assembles into a so-called A-infinity-functor between two curved-dg-categories. In part 2, my collaborator, Jaime Pedregal,  will discuss how one can view this construction as a functor to a Higher Lie groupoid and how one may through exploiting extra structure of this functor, define a notion of higher holonomy.
